## What is Momentum Investing?

Momentum investing is a strategy that capitalizes on the tendency of winning stocks to continue winning and losing stocks to continue losing over a medium-term horizon (typically 6–12 months).

The academic foundation was laid by Jegadeesh and Titman in their landmark 1993 paper, which showed that stocks performing well in the past 12 months tend to outperform over the next 3–12 months.

## Why Momentum Works in India

Indian markets, like most emerging markets, exhibit strong momentum effects for several structural reasons:

1. **Information diffusion is slow** — Retail-dominated markets see delayed price discovery
2. **Earnings momentum** — Strong businesses tend to beat estimates repeatedly
3. **Sectoral rotation** — Themes play out over 12–18 month cycles
4. **Behavioral biases** — Anchoring and loss aversion create underreaction
