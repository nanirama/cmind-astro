    faqs:
      - question: Can NRIs invest in PMS in India?
        answer: Yes, via NRE or NRO accounts; FEMA guidelines apply.
      - question: Can I repatriate returns?
        answer: Yes, returns generated through an NRE account are freely repatriable.
      - question: Do I need to travel to India to open an account?
        answer: No, the entire onboarding process can be completed digitally from anywhere in the world.
      - question: How is my PMS portfolio taxed as an NRI?
        answer: Taxes are deducted at source (TDS) for NRIs as per applicable rates.
      - question: Is the minimum investment the same for NRIs?
        answer: "Yes, the SEBI mandated minimum investment of ₹50 Lakhs applies to NRIs as well."
