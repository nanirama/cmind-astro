# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 1, 2024 | **Version:** 2.0

This Privacy Policy explains how CapitalMind Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. ("CapitalMind", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including name, email, phone number, PAN, and financial information necessary for investment services.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Providing investment advisory and portfolio management services
- Regulatory compliance (SEBI, PMLA, Income Tax)
- Communication and customer support
- Improving our products and services

## 3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and regular security audits.

## 4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@capitalmind.in.

## 5. Contact

For privacy-related queries: privacy@capitalmind.in
