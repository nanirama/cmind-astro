## Strategy Overview

The CapitalMind Momentum Strategy is our flagship quantitative equity strategy. It selects and holds the top 30 stocks from the NSE 500 universe, rebalancing every month based on trailing 12-month price returns (risk-adjusted).

## Investment Philosophy

Markets are not perfectly efficient. Stocks that have performed well over the recent 6–12 months tend to continue outperforming over the next 3–6 months. This "momentum effect" is one of the most robust documented anomalies in finance.

## How It Works

1. **Universe**: NSE 500 (top 500 stocks by market cap)
2. **Ranking**: 12-month return (excluding the last month) adjusted for volatility
3. **Portfolio**: Top 30 stocks, equal-weighted
4. **Rebalancing**: Monthly
5. **Filters**: Liquidity, circuit breaker exclusion, promoter pledge screening

## Risk Profile

This is a **high-risk strategy**. Momentum portfolios can underperform significantly during sharp market reversals. Suitable for investors with:

- Minimum 3-year investment horizon
- High risk tolerance
- Ability to withstand 30–40% drawdowns
